OUR PROGRAMS

Learning Tree Family Child Care offers carefully designed programs for children from 6 weeks through 6 years of age. Each program is thoughtfully crafted to meet the developmental needs of children at different stages, providing appropriate challenges, nurturing support, and opportunities for growth. Our mixed-age classrooms in the preschool years allow younger children to learn from older peers while giving older children opportunities to develop leadership and mentoring skills.

Tailored to Every Stage

1

Infant Program

6 weeks – 12 months

Our infant program provides a safe, clean, and deeply nurturing environment featuring infant-sized furniture, soft mats, floor beds, and carefully selected sensory materials. With a ratio of 1 adult per 3 infants, your baby receives personalized attention and consistent care from the same loving caregivers. We track daily milestones and communicate regularly with parents, ensuring seamless transition to our Toddler program at 18 months.

2

Toddler Program

18 months – 36 months

Our calm, purposeful toddler classrooms foster concentration, self-control, and community learning. Toddlers use child-sized tools to develop independence through preparing snacks, cleaning up, self-care activities, and toilet training. Our language-rich environment supports rapid vocabulary development while preparing children for a smooth transition to our Preschool program.

3

Preschool/

Primary Program

3 – 6 years

Our child-centered, mixed-age classrooms offer individualized activities based on each child's interests and developmental readiness. Children engage with hands-on materials for literacy, math, science, problem-solving, and creativity. We balance self-directed work with collaborative group activities, and our program includes kindergarten preparation for advanced academic skills and social development.
