How to Get Started

Selecting the right childcare for your child is one of the most important decisions you'll make as a parent. We've designed our admissions process to be simple, transparent, and welcoming, making it easier for families to join the Learning Tree community. We encourage you to visit our facility, meet our dedicated staff, observe our classrooms in action, and experience firsthand the warm, nurturing environment where your child will learn and grow. Spaces in our programs fill quickly throughout the year, so we recommend starting the process early to secure your preferred enrollment date.

1

Book a Tour

Schedule a personalized visit to see our classrooms, meet our experienced staff, and experience our nurturing environment firsthand. Ask questions and see how we bring our philosophy to life.

2

Submit Your Application

Complete your online application through Brightwheel and provide all required documentation, and complete the application fee for a seamless enrollment experience. Early submission offers the best opportunity to secure your preferred start date.

3

Secure Your Child's Enrollment

Upon acceptance, a one-month tuition deposit is required to hold your child's spot. This deposit will be applied toward your child's last month of tuition. Payment should be made promptly to guarantee placement.

4

Join Our Community

Connect with us on social media, attend orientation, and become part of the Learning Tree family. Stay informed about events, activities, updates, and your child's daily adventures.
