HOW TO ENROLL

Step 1: Click the Enrollment Button

Click the Enroll in Brightwheel button below and create your parent account in Brightwheel to access the enrollment package.

Step 2: Complete the Forms

Please fill out all required forms and upload any requested documents.

Step 3: Submit & Pay Enrollment Fee

Review your information, submit your forms, and complete the $60 enrollment fee through Brightwheel.

Please note: The enrollment fee is required to process your application and does not guarantee a spot.

Step 4: Enrollment Confirmation

Once your forms are reviewed, we will contact you to confirm availability and your child’s official start date.

What You’ll Need

To make the process easier, please have the following ready:

  • Child’s basic information
  • Parent/guardian contact information
  • Emergency contacts
  • Medical information
  • Required signed forms
  • Inmunizations

Start your Enrollment Process

You’re just a few steps away from joining the Learning Tree family.
Please complete your enrollment through our secure system to get started.

All required forms, documents, and the enrollment fee will be submitted through Brightwheel for a smooth and simple experience.
